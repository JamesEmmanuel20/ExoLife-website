About ExoLife

Bridging the gap between complex astrophysics and public curiosity through immersive web design, procedural graphics, and interactive education.

🌌

Our Mission

Space exploration shouldn't be locked behind academic journals. ExoLife: Beyond Our Sun is built to make astrobiology intuitive, engaging, and accessible to students, educators, and cosmic explorers everywhere.

⚡

Modern Web Standards

Built entirely on high-performance open web technologies without heavy frameworks. Featuring procedural HTML5 Canvas rendering, dual-loading local resiliency, and responsive glassmorphic design.

🔭

Interactive Discovery

From our 50-planet curated catalog and side-by-side Earth comparisons to our live spectroscopy laboratories and transit simulators, every tool is engineered for hands-on learning.
